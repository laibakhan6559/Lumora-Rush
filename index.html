How to Play

Your mission

Guide Lumi, a glowing cosmic orb, along the star road. Gobble star shards to grow bigger, grab moon crystals to buy skins, and reach the Warp Gate at the end of every level. If Lumi's size drops to zero, the light fades.

Controls

  • Touch Drag left or right to steer. Swipe up or tap to jump.
  • Mouse Click and drag to steer. Click without moving to jump.
  • Keyboard ← → or A D to steer, Space or ↑ to jump, P to pause.

Hazards

  • Crystal walls Slip through the gap. Low bars can be jumped.
  • Asteroids Sweep from side to side. Time your run or leap over.
  • Void gaps The road disappears. Jump to cross.
  • Spin blades Wait for a clear moment, or jump the blade.
  • Black holes They tug Lumi inward. Steer away or jump.
  • Laser gates Beams flash on and off. Jump or pass while they sleep.

Power-ups

  • Aegis bubble Absorbs one hit.
  • Star magnet Pulls nearby shards and crystals for a few seconds.

A hit costs 3 size. Bigger Lumi means a bigger safety cushion, but also a bigger target!
